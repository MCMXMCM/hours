## Nocturnale Romanum material

The bundled corpus contains 1,547 GABC chant transcriptions identified as
Nocturnale Romanum material under `GPL-3.0-only`.

- Upstream repository:
  `https://github.com/Nocturnale-Romanum/nocturnale-romanum`
- Pinned upstream revision:
  `84ce1514306be54bf4e693c9e8aa3bfd5e5aa3f2`
- Hours packaging date: 2026-07-24
- Hours modifications: the transcriptions were selected, assigned application
  identifiers and provenance records, combined with other office content, and
  packaged into SQLite. The editable GABC is reproduced separately under
  `Content/Nocturnale-Romanum/gabc`.

The manifest alongside that export records the exact application score ID,
source URL, upstream snapshot checksum, GABC checksum, and filename for every
covered transcription.
