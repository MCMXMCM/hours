Lectio brevis

Benedictio:

May the almighty Lord grant us a quiet night and a perfect end.

Prex poenitentialis

Examen conscientiæ vel Pater Noster totum secreto.

Pater noster, qui es in cælis.

Confíteor Deo omnipoténti.

Canticum: Nunc dimittis

Protect us, Lord, while we are awake.

Now thou dost dismiss thy servant, O Lord.

  1. Quia vidérunt óculi mei * salutáre tuum,

    2. Because my eyes have seen thy salvation,

  2. Quod parásti * ante fáciem ómnium populórum,

    3. Which thou hast prepared before all peoples:

Protect us, Lord, while we are awake.
