Bureå Funeral Chapel Organ — Gedackt 8′ stop

Original stereo pipe-organ samples by Lars Palo.

Upstream distribution:
https://www.purgatorycreek.com/index.php/lars-pipe-organs/

Original project information:
https://familjenpalo.se/vpo/download/

Hours adapts the left and right Korg KMP/KSF multisamples into a single-preset
SoundFont 2 bank. Every chromatically sampled stereo pipe from MIDI 36 through
96 is retained, together with its original sustain-loop metadata. Their levels
are normalized for a consistent teaching guide, and the PCM is repackaged
without lossy compression.
