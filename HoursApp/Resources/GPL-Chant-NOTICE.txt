Hours has modified these transcriptions. Between 2026-07-24 and 2026-10-25
selected scores were given new headers; their adiastematic neume layer,
Euouae, and presentational markup were removed; some were divided, shortened
to the part the Office sings, or set to other words; and all were assigned
application identifiers and provenance records, combined with other office
content, and packaged into SQLite.

The exact corresponding source for this build, including editable GABC files,
checksums, and a fuller account of the modifications, is published with the
Hours source repository:
https://github.com/MCMXMCM/hours
